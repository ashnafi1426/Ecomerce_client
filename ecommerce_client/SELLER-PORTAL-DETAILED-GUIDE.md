## 🏪 ALL 20 SELLER PAGES EXPLAINED

### 1️⃣ **Seller Dashboard** (`/seller/dashboard`)
**What it does:** Your command center - see everything at a glance
- **4 Key Metrics Cards:**
  - Total Revenue with growth percentage
  - Total Orders with trend indicator
  - Active Products + pending approvals count
  - Average Rating with total reviews
- **Recent Orders Table:** Last 10 orders with quick actions (View, Ship, Track)
- **Product Status Table:** Your products with approval status
- **Quick Actions:** Add new product, view all orders
- **Real-time Updates:** Auto-refresh every 30 seconds

**Amazon Equivalent:** Seller Central Home Page

---

### 2️⃣ **Analytics** (`/seller/analytics`)
**What it does:** Deep dive into your business performance
- **Revenue Chart:** 7-day revenue trend with daily breakdown
- **Sales Metrics:** Total sales, orders, average order value
- **Top Products:** Best sellers by revenue
- **Category Performance:** Sales by product category
- **Time Period Filters:** Today, 7 days, 30 days, custom range
- **Export Reports:** Download CSV for accounting

**Amazon Equivalent:** Business Reports

### 3️⃣ **Products** (`/seller/products`)
**What it does:** Manage your entire product catalog
- **Product List:** All products with images, SKU, price, stock
- **Search & Filter:** By name, SKU, category, status
- **Bulk Actions:** Select multiple products for bulk edit/delete
- **Status Badges:** Active, Pending Approval, Inactive, Out of Stock
- **Quick Edit:** Inline price and stock updates
- **Actions:** Edit, View, Duplicate, Delete each product

**Amazon Equivalent:** Manage Inventory

---

### 4️⃣ **Add Product** (`/seller/products/add`)
**What it does:** List new products for sale
- **Product Information:** Name, description, brand, category
- **Pricing:** Regular price, sale price, cost
- **Inventory:** SKU, stock quantity, low stock threshold
- **Images:** Upload multiple product images (drag & drop)
- **Attributes:** Size, color, weight, dimensions
- **SEO:** Meta title, description, keywords
- **Shipping:** Weight, dimensions for shipping calculation
- **Submit for Approval:** Products reviewed before going live

**Amazon Equivalent:** Add a Product

- **Variant Management:** Add/edit product variations

- **Quick Save:** Auto-save drafts

**Amazon Equivalent:** Edit Product Info

### 6️⃣ **Inventory Management** (`/seller/inventory`)
